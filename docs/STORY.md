# Story

**Status: not decided.** The owner picks the premise and the format; until then this file holds the options.
Once picked, this file becomes the canon: premise, characters, places, the rules of the world, and the scene list.

## Premise options (all original; about 30 to 60 minutes of play)

1. **The Night Lift.** You're the overnight lift operator in a tired 1970s concrete tower block. Each stop, a tenant
   steps in with ten floors' worth of conversation. Over one night you learn the building is being sold, and who
   on which floor is hiding what. You choose what to pass along and what to keep.
2. **Last Bus to Halloway.** A night-shift bus driver on the final run before the route is cut. Twelve stops,
   twelve passengers, one breakdown halfway. Small choices about who you wait for change who is still on board at
   the end of the line.
3. **Lost Property.** You run the lost-property office at a city rail station. Each object handed in opens a short
   story about its owner; reuniting them, or not, slowly reveals what happened to the office's last clerk.

## Format options

- Branching text with pictures (a visual novel).
- Point-and-click.
- First-person walk-around in 3D (the current proof of concept).
- Top-down 2D.
